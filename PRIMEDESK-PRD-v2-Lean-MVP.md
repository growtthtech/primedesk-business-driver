**Version:** v2.0 Lean MVP - Updated October 1, 2026

**Category:** Business Process & Digital Technology Guidance Platform

> **PrimeDesk Promise:** You don't need to figure out all the technology. Tell PrimeDesk how your business works, and we'll help you build the right digital system for where you are today - and where you're going next.

## 1. Vision in Simple Words

PrimeDesk helps businesses understand how they work, choose the right digital tools for their current size, see how tools support their work, and know what to improve next.

Long term: PrimeDesk becomes the guidance layer between a business and its technology. Like Uber: Customer need -> Right driver -> Journey -> Status.

PrimeDesk: Business need -> Right technology -> Business process -> Status -> Improvement.

The business does not need to become a technology expert. The product handles the complexity.

## 2. The Problem We Solve

Businesses use many tools separately:
WhatsApp, Instagram, Google Forms, Google Sheets, payment platforms, accounting, CRM, scheduling, email, delivery apps.

But they don't know:
- which tools they really need
- which tools are extra
- if tools work together
- where information gets lost
- which steps are still manual
- where customers get stuck
- what to fix next, and what to leave for later

Gap: **Business operations** vs **Digital technology.** PrimeDesk bridges it.

Core philosophy: **Start where the business is. Build toward where it needs to go.**
A small business may start with WhatsApp + Google Sheets + payment. A bigger one may need CRM + payment + scheduling + accounting + automation. PrimeDesk supports both.

## 3. How PrimeDesk Thinks - The Model

```
BUSINESS
  ↓
BUSINESS PROCESS (how work flows step by step)
  ↓
BUSINESS NEED (what must happen at each step)
  ↓
DIGITAL CAPABILITY (what kind of help is needed - messages, records, payments, scheduling)
  ↓
DIGITAL READINESS (what the business can handle now)
  ↓
RECOMMENDED SOLUTION (simplest good fit for now + what comes later)
  ↓
DIGITAL TOOLS (chosen by owner)
  ↓
RECORDED TOOLS (MVP: owner ticks what they use, no automatic link yet)
  ↓
PROCESS VISIBILITY (see map + what is waiting / stuck)
  ↓
IMPROVEMENT (what to do next)
```

Core Loop: UNDERSTAND -> ASSESS -> RECOMMEND -> ADOPT -> RECORD -> MONITOR (manual) -> IMPROVE -> REASSESS

## 4. Who We Serve First (Narrowed for MVP)

**MVP Focus Only:** Growing service businesses with 2-20 staff who take bookings and payments.

Examples:
- beauty / wellness
- home services / repairs
- private tutors / training
- small clinics

Why them? They share ONE common painful process:
Enquiry > Price/Quote > Payment > Staff Assignment > Service > Follow-up

We do NOT target e-commerce, logistics, real estate, hospitality, large healthcare in MVP. Those come after validation.

Common trait that matters more than industry:
> A business with a repeatable process that has many steps, people, tools, or customer chats.

**Primary User for MVP:** Business Owner only.
Needs to know: how my business works, what tools I need now, what to avoid, what can wait, what needs attention.

Operations Manager, team members, multi-user support: moved to P1.

## 5. Jobs To Be Done

Primary Job:
> When my business depends more on digital tools, I want to know what technology I really need, how it should support my process, and if it is working, so I can improve without extra complexity.

Supporting Jobs:
1. Help me see my process clearly.
2. Help me know what I need at each step.
3. Help me choose tools that fit my size.
4. Help me avoid tools I am not ready for.
5. Help me record which tools I use for which step.
6. Help me see what is working and what is stuck.
7. Help me know what to improve next.

## 6. Key Ideas in Plain Words

- **Business:** The company using PrimeDesk.
- **Process:** Repeatable steps that lead to a result. Example: Customer Booking.
- **Process Stage:** One step. Example: Payment.
- **Business Need:** What must happen. Example: Collect payment before booking.
- **Digital Capability:** Type of help needed. Example: messaging, customer records, payments, scheduling, accounting.
- **Digital Tool:** A product that gives that help. Example: WhatsApp, Sheets, payment platform, CRM.
- **Digital Stack:** All tools the business uses together.
- **Digital Readiness:** What level the business can handle now without stress.
- **Record (MVP word for Connection):** Owner tells us "I use X for this stage." No automatic linking in MVP.

## 7. Digital Readiness - Keep Simple

We never recommend based on what is technically possible. We recommend based on readiness.

**Level 1 - Start:** Mostly manual, WhatsApp, paper, cash. Focus: simple tools, get organized.
**Level 2 - Organize:** Uses a few tools but separately, lots of copying by hand. Focus: standardize, centralize info.
**Level 3 - Connect:** Ready to link tools so info moves by itself. Focus: reduce hand work.
**Level 4 - Optimize:** Mostly digital. Focus: automation, review performance, improve.

MVP shows only the level name + 1 sentence why + simple advice: Start with essentials.

## 8. Recommendation Philosophy

Never say: "You need all these tools."

Always say:
> "Based on how you work today, these are the tools you need now, and these can wait."

Three buckets always:
- **Start Now:** Minimum to solve today's problem (2-4 tools max).
- **Useful Next:** When you grow (example: customer list + scheduling).
- **Advanced Later:** When manual work becomes hard (example: CRM + automation).

Every suggestion must answer: What is it? Why for me? When should I upgrade?

Example:
Requirement: Customer management.
Option A Simple: Sheets / list - Best for few customers.
Option B Growing: CRM - Best for growing volume.
Option C Advanced: CRM + automatic follow-up - Best for high volume.
Explain why, what it solves, when to move.

## 9. Product Principles (Do Not Break)

1. **Process First:** Start with what the business does.
2. **Business Before Technology:** Understand need before suggesting software.
3. **Right-Sized Technology:** Best is not the most advanced. Best is what fits now.
4. **Progressive Adoption:** Start small, upgrade when ready. Owner decides.
5. **Use What Already Works:** Don't force replacement.
6. **Explain Every Recommendation:** Always say why.
7. **Show Business Impact:** Say "12 customers waiting for follow-up, you may lose repeat jobs" not technical errors.
8. **Simplicity:** A non-technical owner must understand in 10 minutes.
9. **Owner Control:** Owner chooses what to adopt, keep, postpone.

PrimeDesk is NOT a CRM, ERP, accounting, payment, or automation tool. It sits above and between them. It is a business-process-first guidance platform.

## 10. MVP Goal - Advice Before Plumbing

**MVP tests ONE question:**
Will an owner describe their process, trust our map and tool plan, and come back?

MVP does NOT test live connections, automation, or analytics.

**MVP Principle to paste on the wall:**
> MVP proves PrimeDesk can understand a process and give right-sized advice. Live tool links come in P1 after validation. In MVP, tools are recorded manually by ticking.

## 11. MVP Journey - 6 Steps Only (Replaces old 18-step flow)

### Screen 1 - Business Setup
Headline: Tell us about your business
Fields:
- Business name
- What do you do? [Beauty/Wellness, Home Services/Repairs, Tutoring/Training, Health/Clinic, Other service]
- How do you work today? [Mostly manual / Few tools / Many tools but not connected / Not sure]
- Team size [1-2 / 3-10 / 11-20]
Button: Continue

### Screen 2 - Pick ONE Process
Headline: What do you want to improve first?
Only 3 templates for MVP:
1. Customer Booking - from first message to booked service
2. Taking Orders + Payment - from order to paid
3. Customer Follow-up - after service to reviews and repeat
Link: My process is different - let me describe it
Button: Continue

### Screen 3 - Describe How You Work
Headline: How does this process work today?
One big box with example: "Customers message on WhatsApp. We ask what they need, send price, they transfer money, we assign staff, do the job, sometimes follow up."
Button: Build My Process

Note for builder: For first 20 users this can be done manually behind the scenes. User does not need to know.

### Screen 4 - Your Process Map
Headline: This is how your business works
Show boxes with arrows:
Enquiry > Price/Quote > Payment > Assign Staff > Do Service > Follow-up
User can rename, delete, + Add stage.
Button: Confirm - This is my process
This confirmation is the magic moment and key metric.

### Screen 5 - Your Plan
Headline: You don't need everything at once
Top box: Your level: ORGANIZE. Why in 1 sentence. Advice: Start with essentials.
Three cards:
- START NOW: WhatsApp Business + Simple list/Sheets + Payment platform you use
- NEXT WHEN YOU GROW: Simple customer list + Scheduling. Add when: >30 customers/week or missed appointments.
- LATER: Full CRM + automatic follow-up. Add when manual follow-up becomes hard.
Each item: checkbox [x] I use this / [ ] Add later + Why this? line.
Button: Build My System

### Screen 6 - My Business Home (3 Tabs Only)
**Tab 1 My Process:** Map + owner-set status per stage: Done / Waiting / Stuck. Example: Follow-up = Stuck - 12 jobs no follow-up.
**Tab 2 My Tools:** List of ticked tools. Each shows: What is it? Why do I have it? Which stage does it help? When to upgrade? Example: WhatsApp for Enquiry - In use. Scheduling - Not added yet.
**Tab 3 My Growth Path:**
TODAY: WhatsApp + List + Payment - Good for now.
NEXT: Customer list + Scheduling - when volume grows.
FUTURE: CRM + automation - when follow-up is hard.

No other tabs in MVP.

## 12. MVP Scope - IN vs OUT

**IN for MVP:**
- Account: Name, Email, Password. Business profile.
- Process: 3 templates, create from words, edit stages, show map.
- Needs: Simple list per stage - Need + Capability. No complex tables.
- Advice: Readiness result, Start Now / Next / Later, simple vs growing options, tick to choose.
- Home: 3 tabs, manual status Done/Waiting/Stuck, Growth Path roadmap.
- Improvement tips in plain words. Example: "Add simple follow-up list."

**OUT for MVP (Move to P1/P2):**
- No real link to WhatsApp, Sheets, Payments. Only manual tick.
- No automatic Working/Needs Attention with %. Only manual Done/Waiting/Stuck.
- No rules like "When paid, move to scheduling."
- No alerts, activity feed, charts, analytics.
- No team members, no multiple processes, no Connection Center.
- No tool marketplace, no comparison engine, no advanced automation.

## 13. P1 - After Validation (Only if MVP metrics pass)

- One real link only: e.g., record payment to mark Payment Done, or simple customer form to customer list.
- Simple health: Waiting too long, Missing follow-up.
- Reminders: "12 jobs have no follow-up."
- Add 2nd process, staff roles, pause/reconnect for that one link.
- More templates (5-7 total).

## 14. P2 - Future

- Many links, advanced automation through process ("What should happen automatically?").
- Performance numbers, benchmarking, business-wide map, predictive tips, industry packs.

## 15. What PrimeDesk Will NOT Do (v1 + v2)

Will not replace CRM, accounting, payment processors, messaging, scheduling, inventory, ERP, project tools. PrimeDesk sits above and between them.

## 16. MVP Success Criteria (7 - Replaces old 19)

MVP passes if an owner can:
1. Create account and finish setup in under 10 minutes without help.
2. Pick 1 process and see a correct map.
3. Edit map and confirm "Yes, this is my business."
4. See level + understand why in 1 sentence.
5. Receive Start Now plan with 2-4 tools max.
6. Choose what to keep now vs later.
7. Return after 7 days to view Growth Path.

Measure:
- % finish setup (target 70%+)
- % confirm map is correct (target 70%+)
- % accept Start Now plan (target 50%+)
- % return in 7 days (target 30%+ for early test)
- Qualitative: "Would you pay / refer?" in interviews with 20 users.

## 17. How to Test Before Building More

1. Paper/Figma test with 5-10 owners using beauty salon example. Show Screen 4 + 5 on paper. Ask: Is this your process? Is advice correct?
2. Concierge test with 20 users: Let them do 6 steps, you create map + advice manually behind scenes.
3. Build only Wizard + Map + Advice web version. Track completion.
4. Only then build one live link.

## 18. Example for Testing - Beauty Salon

Input: "Clients DM on Instagram/WhatsApp, we ask service, send price list, they transfer, we book time in notebook, assign stylist, do service, rarely follow up."
Map: Enquiry > Quote > Payment > Booking > Staff Assign > Service > Follow-up
Level: ORGANIZE - uses chat + notebook + transfers but copies by hand.
Start Now: WhatsApp Business + Simple customer list + Payment / transfers record.
Next: Shared booking calendar + customer list.
Later: CRM + automatic reminders.
